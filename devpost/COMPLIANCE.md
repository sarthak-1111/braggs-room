## How it's judged (Rules §6)

Stage 1 is pass/fail: the project reasonably applies the required tools and meets all the requirements above. Stage 2 has four criteria, weighted equally. Ties are broken in this order:

1. **Design**: a complete, coherent product experience, not just a technical proof of concept.
2. **Technical Implementation**: how well it's built, and how well it uses CLAD, Lens Studio and the Lens Studio MCP.
3. **Innovation/Idea**: how creative and novel it is, and how it differs from existing concepts.
4. **Presentation**: the video shows it working end to end, and the pitch says what problem it solves, who it's for and why it matters.
